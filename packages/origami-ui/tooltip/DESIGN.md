# Tooltip design map

Ownership and read timing between parts. Usage is in the [README](README.md); the contract is SPEC section 18. Native presentation and Escape/outside participation run in the package-private floating and layer pieces mapped in [popover/DESIGN.md](../popover/DESIGN.md); their machines run in effects, render hooks and events and aren't mapped here.

## Refresh dependencies

- A timer, grace-area, group or intent write goes through the effective `open` signal; Presence, the floating piece and the Trigger re-render from it.
- The Trigger's association depends on the render that follows Content's registration.
- A Tooltip in Popover Content activates in the render after the Popover's reveal commit, which re-runs its floating piece's `effect` through the parent's readiness signal.
- `TooltipIntent` learns of every close (its own, Escape, outside, the application, the group) from the floating piece's `closed` hook, which runs when synchronization observes the closed intent; there it cancels a pending open and ends a transit, and the group drops it if it is still current, warming only when the replaced intent was its own open.
