## Refresh dependencies

- The Grid's `aria-labelledby` reads the Heading's registration. A Heading after the Grid, or in a child component, registers after the Grid's host bindings ran; the registration changes a signal they read, so Angular refreshes the Grid again in the same change detection, also on the server.
- A move or a choice into another month, year or page writes `month` (or the value the display follows) and a focus request; a drill-down or Escape writes `view` (and `month`) and a request. The new period's or view's cells register in the render that shows them, and the old ones are destroyed in it; only then does the after-render effect find the target and focus it.
